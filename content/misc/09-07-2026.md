## VAPID Goals

A short list of more fuzzy, abstract goals that I am explicitly putting as
VAPID (CPG Grey!). I actually find nothing _wrong_ with VAPID goals - as you
mature and tackle larger and larger problems, the abstraction is an advantage
to not get bogged down on a single solution or path. 

For me, the issue with VAPID goals is the stress - I know they need to be done,
I just don't know when or how to do them. Rapid fire:

- Figure out better communication I'm a very not information dense person,
  I kind of just yap without consideration. Which is a step up from saying
  _nothing_. But not great. Maybe need to up empathy and think about what
  other people want.

- Figure out long term work prioritization - I suck at long term goals and
  working coherently towards them. I tend to leverage the harness of my
  interests - that takes me far when it works. But I think I need a bit more
  structure, especially for something like work.

- Health. This is not so VAPID. There are gyms nearby, healthy eating is not
  hard for me - I just stopped cooking because I'm out of energy and stressing
  about my career. I also have yet to get a physical or dentist appointment
  this year - stop that, book it.

- Reading books. I have _so_ many physical books and even more PDFs that I've
  yet to read. Gotta read.
