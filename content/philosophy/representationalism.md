---
title: Representationalism
date: 2026-10-01
---

Representationalism might be the tendency to state a thought, notice the
meta-constraints, describe them, and then keep recursively trying to go higher
and higher.

Heidegger, mystics, etc. would be the people who refuse to try and recursively
redescribe, but try and focus on the weird nature of getting into the "first"
statement in the first place.

Inferentialism is orthogonal to both. I don't really even think a thought such
as "your preconceived notions constrain what you can think" is a central problem
in inferentialism. It's almost like, given that words are a given, how do they
relate to one another. It's not necesarily trying to answer the "prior" space to
words, that either Heidegger or Representationalism inevitably try to do & start
navel gazing.
