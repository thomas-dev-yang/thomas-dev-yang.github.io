a "bonus".

I did competitive programming and studied some TCS on my own time. I'm very

will kick you in the ass if you don't accept _its_ constraints.

I have _no clue_ how I'll get actually better at the high level concepts here
besides exercises and reading, but I will be expending non-trivial effort in
this area.
